You are set up.

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or DeepSeek, type a prompt, and press Ask all in the small launcher at the bottom right of the page. The same prompt goes to the others you switched on, in the tabs you are already signed in to.

Where your AI time goes

Every minute in an AI tab is one of three things: writing a prompt, waiting for the answer, or reading it. Timed from page events, never from what you type.
